## Trigger

Real PiDog HIL bench step 10 printed `_rgb_strip_thread Exception: Third argument must be a list of at least one, but not more than 32 integers`; the strip did not light, but the bench still reported the step COMPLETE because the vendor swallowed the exception in a background thread.

## Routing

Routed to Device because the defect was a real-hardware RGB issue under `src/device/`, which Device owns per routing.

## Root Cause

Vendor `pidog/rgb_strip.py` `monochromatic()` is the only RGB style that omits the `int()` cast used by the other five styles. Float brightness yields float RGB values rejected by the ws2812/SPI driver. Because `BoardPort.set_rgb` exposes `brightness: float = 1.0`, every monochromatic call failed on hardware, including the default.

## Fix

Device added an adapter-layer workaround in `PidogBoardAdapter.set_rgb`: for monochromatic only, pre-scale channels to clamped integers and pass integer `brightness=1`; the other five styles remain unchanged.
