- Issue #12 tracks UD-02: whether v1 should be stationary or include walking motion. This influences M3/M6 scope and the motion-safety architecture. Acceptance criteria include a recorded decision in architecture docs and linked tracking.

Options:
1) Stationary v1 (Recommended)
   - Pros: Lower engineering and safety risk, faster to certify, smaller scope for M3/M6
   - Cons: Reduced capability; may delay mobility-related features until later milestones

2) Walking v1
   - Pros: Higher capability for end-users; accelerates mobility features and testing
   - Cons: Increased safety/engineering risk, longer development, requires motion-safety architecture and validation

Recommendation:
- Recommend Option 1: Stationary v1. It reduces risk, keeps the initial release achievable, and defers walking motion to M3/M6 where safety and systems engineering can be fully planned.
